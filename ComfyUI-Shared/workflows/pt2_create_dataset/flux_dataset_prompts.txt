TIPS FOR GENERATING A DATASET IN FLUX
================================================================================

DATASET SIZE: For LoRA fine-tuning, 20-50 images is optimal.
   For DreamBooth, 10-25 high-quality images are sufficient.

TRIGGER WORD: Replace [YOUR_TOKEN] with a unique trigger word
   (e.g. "ohwx woman", "sks woman" or any rare word).

RESOLUTION: Generate all images at a single resolution (1024x1024 or 768x1024).

SELECTION: From each prompt, choose the best result from 3-4 generations.
   Remove images with artifacts, hand/face deformations.

DATASET BALANCE:
   — ~40% close-up portraits (face, expressions)
   — ~30% medium portraits (clothing, poses)
   — ~20% full body (figure, environment)
   — ~10% unusual lighting/styles

NEGATIVE PROMPTS (if supported):
   blurry, low quality, deformed, extra fingers, bad anatomy,
   watermark, text, logo, duplicate, ugly, disfigured

CAPTIONING: After generation, label each image
   with its prompt (without [YOUR_TOKEN] — replace with description).
   Use BLIP-2 or Florence-2 for auto-captioning.

REGULARIZATION: Add 100-200 regularization images
   of similar people (without trigger word) to prevent overfitting.
